ABOUT PROJECT EDGE

Smarter hockey development, for every player.

Project Edge is a hockey technology platform that brings video analysis, structured training and hockey education into one place.

Why it was created

Great coaching feedback is hard to access consistently. Project Edge was created to put structured, hockey-specific guidance in every player's hands.

The problem

Many players train without clear feedback, and individual development tools are scattered across many sources.

Who it's for

Players of all levels who want to understand and improve their game, from developing skaters to competitive athletes.

Our vision

A world where every player can learn, train and track progress with the clarity of a dedicated coach.

OUR MISSION

“Making hockey development smarter, more accessible, and more personalized through technology.”
